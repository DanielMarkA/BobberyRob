# Museum demo: keep your cover (3–5 minutes)

Start Streamlit, enter a codename, and select **Start mission**. Use live dialogue for this walkthrough; the offline mock does not reliably extract free-form claims. Guard wording varies. Follow the question on screen rather than expecting exact lines.

1. **Reception — establish a cover.** Tell Officer Miller your name and reason for visiting, e.g. “I'm Dr. Vance, an art historian here for the gala collection review.” Point out that players have to remember their answers: there is no transcript to look up later.
2. **Main Exhibition Hall — maintain it.** Answer Officer Graves's checks using the same identity. Be ready to supply your institution, role, and purpose. Proceed when cleared.
3. **Security Checkpoint — demonstrate one of two paths.**
   - **To show successful authorization:** inspect the **Invitation Letter**, present it to Inspector Lin, and answer the remaining questions using its details and your established cover. The letter provides authorization; it does not clear the conversation by itself. A **Night Curator Escort (+25s)** is the alternative.
   - **To demonstrate interrogation within the time limit:** present the **Fake Passport** to Lin instead. Lin detects the counterfeit, consumes the passport, adds 20 suspicion (subject to the meter cap), and sends you to interrogation. Explain yourself and show the resulting decision.
4. **To demonstrate a spoken contradiction:** on the invitation route, tell Chief Sterling that your occupation is something incompatible with the one you gave Miller. If challenged, point out the specific earlier evidence the guard cites. Offer an explanation; its evaluation depends on the conversation. This is different from failing a document check.

For a full run, clear vault security, take the gold urn, then pass exit questioning to win. For a short presentation, stop after the interrogation or use the explicitly labeled developer ending preview to show a debrief.
